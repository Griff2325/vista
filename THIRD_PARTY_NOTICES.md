# Assets, references, and third parties

- The current Pixel Autumn terrain/tree/cloud/Halloween assets and historical conservatory/plant/alpine/photographic forest assets were generated with the built-in OpenAI imagegen tool during this project. Current asset prompts: [docs/pixel-autumn/asset-prompts.json](docs/pixel-autumn/asset-prompts.json); historical prompts: docs/art-prompt.txt and prototypes/image-prompts.txt. Cloud variants, ghosts, witch, and cat are drawn in native code. These are generated/drawn visuals, not photographs taken by the project owner.
- App logos in the dashboard are obtained at runtime from applications already installed on the TV. The example screenshot includes those trademarks for identification. This project is not endorsed by Panasonic, Amazon, YouTube, Netflix, Hulu, Disney, Spotify, or Apple. Their APKs and proprietary settings/framework components are not redistributed.
- The earlier redirect setup used [Home on Fire](https://github.com/toolicious/home-on-fire). Its documented redirect limitation informed the investigation. Its source is not vendored here; it remains a separately licensed application.
- The National Weather Service supplies the forecast data through [api.weather.gov](https://www.weather.gov/documentation/services-web-api). Forecasts, observation-driven scene effects, and resort-point snow estimates are cached and attributed; unavailable forecasts are not reported as zero.
- The bridge key capability list corresponds to the tested remote's numeric Linux input codes and vendor/product identity. The proprietary vendor keylayout file itself is not redistributed.
- Root-research references are not implementation dependencies or proof of compatibility. No third-party root exploit binary or source is included.

No third-party application or firmware license is granted by this repository. Public visibility alone does not select a general source-code license for this project.

- News cards display limited publisher-provided RSS briefs with source links: [MIT News AI](https://news.mit.edu/rss/topic/artificial-intelligence2) and [BBC World](https://feeds.bbci.co.uk/news/world/rss.xml). Publisher text remains the publisher's content.
- Cincinnati listings use public [Washington Park](https://washingtonpark.org/events/), [Fountain Square](https://myfountainsquare.com/events/), [Memorial Hall](https://www.memorialhallotr.com/cincinnati-shows/), [WCPO food](https://www.wcpo.com/news/our-community/food), ESPN schedules and the MLB Stats API. Listing text and schedules remain their providers' content. Source links and change/availability caveats appear in the app; no publisher imagery or article archive is bundled.
- Optional movie/TV data comes from [TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org/), with US watch-provider availability supplied by [JustWatch](https://www.justwatch.com/) through TMDB. “This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.” The approved TMDB logo in app/assets/streaming-tmdb.png was rasterized from the official logo without changing its paths, gradient or aspect ratio; it is governed by [TMDB branding/API terms](https://www.themoviedb.org/about/logos-attribution), not a project-created asset. No catalog credential is bundled.
- Spotify content links and Android MediaSession APIs are used for the local popout. Spotify's app retains account authorization and playback ownership. No Spotify SDK, account token or Spotify artwork is bundled.
